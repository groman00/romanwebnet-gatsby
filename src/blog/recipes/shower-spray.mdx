Make your own simple shower spray to fight off mold and mildew.

{/* excerpt end */}

## Ingredients

- 3 parts water
- 1 part vinegar
- 5-10 drops essential oils
- Spray bottle


## Directions

- Using the provided ratios, fill your spray bottle with water and vinegar.
- Add your favorite essential oils, such as eucalyptus or lavender.  You can mix oils, or just use one.
- After every shower, spray down the walls and floor to prevent bacteria from growing.
